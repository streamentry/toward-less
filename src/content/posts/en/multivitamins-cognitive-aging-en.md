A 2024 analysis of three randomized COSMOS cognitive substudies included 5,203 distinct older participants. The pooled benefit was 0.07 standard deviations for overall cognition (95% CI 0.03–0.11) and 0.06 for episodic memory. The overall cognition analysis included two substudies. Researchers estimated that the effect corresponded to about two years of age-related cognitive change. This was a statistical comparison, not a measurement of biological brain age.

The clinic substudy alone did not establish a statistically significant benefit for overall cognition or executive function, although episodic memory improved relative to placebo. The tested formulation was Centrum Silver; other products, younger adults and dementia prevention remain uncertain. The findings come from one parent trial, not three independent replications.

A standard-dose multivitamin may be reasonable for older adults, especially when dietary adequacy is uncertain. Review medications and existing supplements with a clinician, and avoid unnecessary high doses. Prioritize exercise, adequate nutrition, sleep and vascular health.

**A modest cognitive benefit is worth investigating. It is not a proven way to reverse aging or prevent Alzheimer's disease.**

[Vyas et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition (2024), DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2023.12.011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11103094/)
