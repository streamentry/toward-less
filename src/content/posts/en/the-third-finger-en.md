A social post uses a two-finger metaphor for determination: willfulness and discipline squeeze a melon seed. Paul Graham’s 2009 essay adds ambition as a third element and discusses limits to the metaphor.

## Graham’s model

- Willfulness is the drive to pursue a goal. Graham suggests it is partly innate; will alone can become self-indulgence.
- Discipline constrains that drive. He argues it can be cultivated.
- Ambition chooses the destination. Graham thinks it can grow through experience and time with ambitious peers.
- He also notes limits: success can bring new temptations, too much discipline may suppress initiative, and enjoying the work can reduce the need for determination.

## What this supports

Graham describes his experience trying to predict startup success at Y Combinator; this is not a controlled study. The post’s two-finger image omits ambition. Treat the model as a prompt for reflection, not a proven formula for success.
