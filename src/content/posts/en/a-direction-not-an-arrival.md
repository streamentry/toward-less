Toward Less is a place for notes on technology, family, and Buddhist practice.

The name is a direction, not a claim to have arrived.

## Less of what?

Less unnecessary complexity. Less distraction. Less clinging to ideas simply because they are mine.

Not less responsibility, care, or useful work. Some things deserve more: patience, honest attention, time with the people we love, and the courage to do what is necessary.

## A place to return to

I want this notebook to leave room for mistakes, changed beliefs, and unfinished questions. A useful piece of writing need not make its author look certain. It should help someone see or act a little more clearly.

Technology, ordinary life, and practice belong together here. The same question runs through them: what is useful, what is unnecessary, and what am I holding too tightly?

**Less clinging. Clearer seeing.**
