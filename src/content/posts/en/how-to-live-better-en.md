Advice can become one more list of things you are supposed to do. [HowToLiveBetter](https://github.com/streamentry/HowToLiveBetter) takes a different form: a menu of 650 recommendations, not a checklist. The README says to choose one or two; the author admits not having followed most of them.

Each entry lays out the cost in money, time, energy, or effort, what it may return, how strong the evidence is, and where that evidence comes from. The site lets readers search and filter by topic, evidence grade, and cost.

The grades need context. An A means a specific, traceable result from sources such as a meta-analysis, a large cohort, or a trial. It does not automatically establish causation. Legal, insurance, and public-benefit guidance follows mainland China’s rules, so it may not apply elsewhere. The Chinese edition is the source; English and Vietnamese translations may lag.
