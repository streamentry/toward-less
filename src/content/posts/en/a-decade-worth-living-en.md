Protect your health. Move daily, build strength, sleep well, eat simply, avoid tobacco, and seek appropriate preventive care. 

Build useful skills, learn to sell ethically, use AI without surrendering judgment, finish fewer projects well, and create assets that endure. Keep financial reserves, diversify, avoid destructive debt, and define enough.

Be present with your children, partner and parents. Apologize when wrong. Give attention rather than merely providing money. These years cannot be recovered.

Protect your mornings. Create more than you consume. Test ideas against reality. Change your mind when evidence changes. Return when you drift.

Practice generosity, ethical conduct and mindfulness. Observe craving before obeying it. Do not make spiritual progress another competition. Leave useful knowledge and tools behind.

Build wealth without becoming dependent on wealth. Build capability without needing to prove yourself. Build freedom without postponing contentment.

**Measure the decade by the quality of ordinary days and the suffering you help reduce.**
