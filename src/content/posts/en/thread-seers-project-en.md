At the center of [The Thread Seers](https://streamentry.github.io/thethreadseers.com/) is an image: invisible threads connect all living things. The seven-book fantasy series follows Lyra Chen from age twelve to eighteen as she discovers what those threads mean.

The site is both an introduction and a place to read. Book One, _The Awakening Thread_, is available online. Readers can also explore the Thread Dimension, the series’ magic system and traditions, a glossary, and project updates.

The [repository](https://github.com/streamentry/thethreadseers.com) shows how the site is put together: a reader, book and chapter pages, world-building, and updates, with narrative content stored in Markdown. The code and tools use the MIT license. The manuscript, characters, lore, artwork, and audiobook recordings remain copyrighted; free online reading does not grant reuse rights.

The project brings together a story about connection and a site that makes its world easier to explore. Its central image is simple; the work behind the site is open to inspect, while the story itself remains its author’s work.
