The Thread Seers is a seven-book fantasy series following Lyra Chen from age 12 to 18 through a story about invisible threads connecting living things.

The site offers:

- Book One free to read online in English and Vietnamese;
- a guide to the Thread Dimension, the series’ magic system and traditions;
- a glossary and project updates.

The repository says the code and tools use the MIT license. The manuscript, characters, world lore, artwork, and audiobook recordings remain copyrighted; free reading does not grant reuse rights.
