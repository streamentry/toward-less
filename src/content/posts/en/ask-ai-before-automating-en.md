Before asking AI to speed up a task, ask whether the task is worth doing.

A screenshot attributed to Prompter suggests asking for a better way to reach a goal, including steps that may be automated. One more question helps: what can be removed before automating anything?

Give AI:

- your goal and constraints;
- the steps you take now.

Then ask for the simplest path, what to skip, and one assumption to test today. AI can suggest options; real use must test them. A fluent answer does not show that the work or product is needed.
