We often choose a familiar approach, then ask AI to speed it up. But what if **the approach itself is not worth pursuing**?

In a post on X, Prompter (@PromptLLM) suggests asking AI for a more effective way to solve a problem—including steps we have not realized could be automated.

I would take that one step further: don't just ask *what can be automated*. Ask **what can be eliminated**.

Instead of automating a thousand cold emails, perhaps find ten people who genuinely need to buy. Instead of building an app faster, perhaps first find out who would pay.

Ask AI: *“My goal is X, with constraints Y. What's the simplest path? What should I skip? Which assumption should I test today?”*

AI can be wrong. Let it widen the options; let reality test the answers.

**Speed cannot save work that never needed doing.**
