Buddhist Textual Witnesses is a source-aligned catalog for comparing Buddhist texts without treating parallel passages as copies of one recoverable original.

The catalog records works, languages, witnesses, and exact source revisions across:

- Sanskrit, Buddhist Hybrid Sanskrit, and Prakrit/Gāndhārī;
- Classical Chinese;
- Pāli as an alignment baseline when useful, not the root of every tradition.

It keeps manuscripts, editions, translations, and reconstructions distinct. Its crosswalks follow three safeguards:

- a full textual parallel is not necessarily an exact parallel;
- machine-generated matches remain candidates until human review and explicit promotion;
- sources with unclear redistribution rights may be registered without being mirrored.

The project is comparison infrastructure, not a claim that one reconstructed Buddhist canon exists.
