Before building, check three things:

## Commitment

- praise is not demand. For a weekly project evening, try one date and see who shows up and returns before booking six weeks.

## First-use barriers

- a meetup for people who find it hard to approach strangers should help with introductions, not require each newcomer to solve that problem alone.

## The skill behind the feature

- in a coding app meant to teach debugging, offer a hint before fixing an error. Someone who wants speed may prefer an automatic fix.

These are design questions, not proof that a particular product will succeed.
