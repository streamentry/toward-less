In his 2015 essay [*The Days Are Long but the Decades Are Short*](https://blog.samaltman.com/the-days-are-long-but-the-decades-are-short), Sam Altman reminds us that life is not a rehearsal. Time is limited. Make it count.

It is easy to turn that into another command: do more, achieve more, waste nothing.

But a life spent proving its worth can become a life we barely inhabit.

## What actually counts?

An afternoon with your child produces no achievement badge.

A walk needs no destination. Dinner with family needs no optimization. A day of rest need not justify itself.

These are not gaps between the important things. **They are the important things.**

Few of us will be remembered centuries after we die. But remembrance is not the only measure of a life. Care can ripple through other people without preserving our names.

Making life count is not the same as filling a calendar.

Some things deserve urgency. Some deserve refusal. Some moments ask only for our presence.

**Do not wait for another life to live this one.**
