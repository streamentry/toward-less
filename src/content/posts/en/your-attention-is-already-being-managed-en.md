## Attention and its limits

Attention matters because it shapes what we notice and what we do next. But a sentence about attention does not establish a direct link to life quality or prove that feeds rewire a person’s brain. Health, work, family, money, and environment also shape what choices are available.

## A small practice

- Notice what is pulling your attention by default.
- Choose one task or person for the next block of time.
- Change one avoidable cue, such as leaving your phone out of reach.

These are practical choices, not a promise to control every thought or circumstance. When attention wanders, return without treating distraction as a moral failure.
