[Saigon2Hanoi](https://streamentry.github.io/saigon2hanoi/) preserves a Vietnamese personal blog in a static reading site. It rebuilds the site from a September 2007 Wayback Machine capture and later pages that survived elsewhere. The landing capture has ten posts dated 23 August to 1 September 2007. The archive expands the restoration to 68 captured article records across the blog and later migrations, each with its own page.

[ARCHIVE.md](https://github.com/streamentry/saigon2hanoi/blob/main/ARCHIVE.md) and the asset manifest document the capture chain and image sources. The site has no build step or runtime dependencies. It does not recreate the old WordPress testimonial endpoint, and no form data leaves the browser. It preserves the pages that could be recovered, with their source trail visible.
