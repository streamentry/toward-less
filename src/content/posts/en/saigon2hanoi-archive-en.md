Saigon2Hanoi restores a Vietnamese personal blog as a static reading site and records which parts could be recovered.

- The landing index uses the 2 September 2007 /blog/ capture. The requested root capture redirects there; a 4 September capture checks continuity. The index shows ten posts dated 23 August–1 September.
- The full archive contains 68 captured article records, each on its own page: 51 from the 2007 blog layer and 17 from later migrations.
- The asset manifest records image sources and missing items; unavailable images are not fabricated.

The README says the site has no build step or runtime dependencies. It does not recreate the old WordPress testimonial endpoint, and the form sends no data.
