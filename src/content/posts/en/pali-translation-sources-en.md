I’m building [Kinh Tạng Pāli Việt](https://streamentry.github.io/kinh-tang-pali/), a Pāli-to-Vietnamese translation project. I want a reader to see not only how a passage reads in Vietnamese, but which text it follows and where the translation sits among its references.

The project pins a SuttaCentral/Bilara Pāli edition and links passages by segment ID. It keeps the Pāli source, English references, the existing Vietnamese translation when available, and the project’s new Vietnamese text as distinct layers. When a reference is missing, the gap is recorded. English passages written by the project are labeled as project translations.

One content source feeds the website, EPUB, and PDF. The catalog covers MN1–MN152, while each translation moves through editorial stages. A catalog entry is not, by itself, a finished or published translation.

AI helps propose translations, compare the layers, check consistency, and support quality review. It is a tool, not an authority. When references differ, the project returns to its pinned Pāli text. A published status means the work passed the project’s stated quality gate; it does not claim independent scholarly certification.
