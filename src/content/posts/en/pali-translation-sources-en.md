Kinh Tạng Pāli Việt places a new Vietnamese translation beside its pinned Pāli source and reference layers, so readers can see which text each passage follows.

Each passage keeps separate:

- the pinned SuttaCentral/Bilara Pāli text, linked by segment ID;
- English references, including Bhikkhu Sujato where available;
- the existing Vietnamese translation by Venerable Thích Minh Châu where available;
- the project’s new Vietnamese translation.

The pinned Pāli edition is the project’s source text, not a claim about a single historical original. The catalog covers MN 1–152, but a catalog entry is not a finished translation. One content source feeds the website, EPUB, and PDF.

AI helps propose translations and check consistency. It is a tool, not an authority. A published status means the text passed the project’s quality gate; it does not mean every segment received independent scholarly review.

Sources: [Kinh Tạng Pāli Việt README](https://github.com/streamentry/kinh-tang-pali/blob/main/README.md) and [quality assessments](https://github.com/streamentry/kinh-tang-pali/blob/main/docs/quality-assessments.md) (accessed 14 November 2026).
