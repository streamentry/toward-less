A saved X thread offers eight ways to lower blood pressure and includes two Amazon product cards. I could not reload the thread in this revision, so this article paraphrases the archived text and cannot verify whether the links were paid.

- **Guideline care:** The 2025 ACC/AHA guideline includes healthy eating, activity and medication when indicated. These are standard recommendations, not secrets.
- **Supplements:** Cochrane found garlic may lower blood pressure, but its review had only two trials and 87 people, with imprecise results and no cardiovascular outcomes. NCCIH describes garlic evidence as small or low quality, and omega-3 results as mixed. No supplement has shown effects comparable to hypertension drugs.
- **Magnesium:** A 2026 meta-analysis included 78 RCTs overall; 55 trials with 3,011 participants reported blood pressure. Average differences were −2.50 mmHg systolic and −1.58 mmHg diastolic. The authors call the effects modest and clinical relevance uncertain.
- **Nitric oxide:** In the saved thread, one item attributes higher blood pressure to more available nitric oxide. Endothelial nitric oxide relaxes blood vessels; that biology does not prove an oral supplement treats hypertension.

The archived text also gives a 10%-per-decade estimate for nitric oxide decline, but I could not verify its source. Product cards are not evidence. Use guideline-based care; do not replace prescribed treatment with supplements.
