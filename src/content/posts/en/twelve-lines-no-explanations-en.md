Klöss’s X post asks Claude Fable 5.2 for advice for the next ten years, with no explanations. It returns twelve aphorisms. Anthropic’s announcement confirms the model’s launch, not the quality of its advice.

## What is useful

- The line about writing down beliefs and rereading them creates a feedback loop.
- The advice to learn the frontier and serve people beyond it is a market hypothesis worth testing.
- The reminder to keep human judgment when using agents is a useful constraint.

## What is missing

The list gives no reasons, trade-offs, or stop conditions. Cutting projects can limit exploration; keeping costs low can conflict with useful investment; saying no faster can close valuable options. Context changes each answer.

Two lines do refer to the decade: invest deeply in a few relationships over those years, and make things you will still be proud of in 2036. Most of the list is timeless. It is a prompt, not a forecast or a strategy.

## Make one line testable

Ask:

- Why does this fit my situation?
- What does it cost or rule out?
- What evidence would show it is working—or wrong?

The list can start the thinking. The judgment remains yours.
