I smoked for more than ten years and tried many ways to quit. None worked for me. This is a personal account, not a treatment guide.

## A different question

I asked ChatGPT whether there was another effective option. It introduced Tabex, a medicine containing cytisine, and gave me a structured course to consider. I acted on that suggestion.

## What the evidence says

Cytisine is not a new or untested idea. WHO’s 2024 guideline recommends it as one medication option for adults who want to stop using tobacco. A 2023 Cochrane review found moderate-certainty evidence that cytisine helped more people quit than placebo; its evidence search ran through April 2022.

## What changed for me

At the time of publication, on 8 November 2026, I had been smoke-free for more than 90 days. I still cannot separate the effects of the medicine, the plan, and my readiness at that moment.

## What this story does not prove

- ChatGPT is not a clinician.
- Tabex is not suitable for everyone, and access or approval differs by country.
- One person’s outcome does not show that the medicine alone caused it or predict what will happen next.

Nicotine dependence is not simply a thought that more willpower can erase. Finding an evidence-based option helped me act without turning the struggle into more self-blame.

Do not copy an AI-generated medication schedule. Check the official leaflet and speak with a qualified clinician or pharmacist about treatment and safety.
