A viral account claims secret superintelligence, a solution to P versus NP, and connected cyberattacks. None of those claims should be treated as established merely because they fit a compelling story. Even a proof that P equals NP would not guarantee practical algorithms: polynomial running times can be enormous, and physical resources and experimental feedback still matter. AI helping researchers improve models is also different from autonomous, sustained recursive self-improvement. Cyber incidents require independent attribution; timing alone establishes no connection. The sensible response is to prepare for rapid technological change without betting everything on a particular timeline. Build transferable skills, maintain financial resilience, secure accounts and backups, and preserve human authority over consequential decisions. In Buddhist practice, heedfulness (appamāda) means attending to conditions without surrendering to fear. **Take progress seriously. Demand evidence. Do not let an imagined future consume the life you have today.**
